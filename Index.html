ULTIMATRIX


What is it?


The Ultimatrix is a device that allows the user to transform into various alien species. It is an upgraded version of the Omnitrix, which was created by Azmuth, the smartest Galvan in the universe. The Ultimatrix has the ability to evolve the user's alien forms into their ultimate versions, granting them enhanced powers and abilities.
